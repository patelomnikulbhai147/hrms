ZeniaHR - Module Demo
=====================

This folder contains two demo screens from the ZeniaHR platform.

   1. payroll.html             - Payroll Management
   2. invoice-management.html  - Invoice Management

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Double-click either file. It opens in your web browser
(Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

Nothing needs to be installed. No login, no internet connection
and no setup is required.

WHAT YOU CAN TRY
----------------
Payroll
   - Search for an employee, or filter by branch / department
   - Click any row's eye icon to see the full salary breakdown
     (earnings, deductions, net salary)
   - Tick employees to see the payroll workflow scope update
   - Try "Generate Payroll", "Apply Bonus" and "Export"

Invoice Management
   - Move between the tabs at the top
   - Search invoices, or filter by status
   - Click any invoice row to open it - customer details,
     line items, GST breakdown and payment history
   - Try "Record Payment" - the totals and status update live
   - Try "Print / Download" for the printable tax invoice

NOTE
----
This is a demonstration using sample data. The figures, employee
names and company names shown are examples only.

For the best experience, view on a laptop or desktop screen.
